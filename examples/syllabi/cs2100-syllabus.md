## Policies

**Late work.** Homework submitted up to 24 hours late loses 20 percent. Nothing is accepted
after 24 hours. Each student has two free 24-hour extensions for the semester; ask on Ed
before the deadline.

**Collaboration and AI tools.** You may discuss ideas with classmates, but every line of code
you submit must be your own. Generative AI tools may be used to explain concepts but not to
write code you submit for homework, labs or exams.

**Attendance.** Lab attendance is required; a missed lab scores zero unless excused.

**Regrades.** Regrade requests go through Gradescope within one week of grades being released.
